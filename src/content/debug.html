ACM EXTENDED / MISSION TOOLS

Debug menu reference

Read every field in the ACME debug overlay, distinguish live patient values from diagnostic defaults, and identify which system needs checking.

Start with the patient and the units

The overlay exposes game state for testing and troubleshooting. It includes patient measurements, internal model scores, system switches and machine-wide counters. A green value is a display classification; it does not establish that the patient is stable.

Enable Addon Options > ACM Extended: Debug > Overlay > Debug menu. This is a client-side option. The same group contains the text-scale setting and visibility switches for optional sections.

Check the target

The name at the top identifies the patient being inspected. A treatment can change that target.

Read the whole label

Eff under Vitals is volume. Eff under Pressors is support. CPP under Acidosis is a deficit fraction, not a pressure.

Recognize defaults

n/a means unavailable. no TBI state means no TBI state record. Many numeric fields fall back to zero when their state has not been populated.

Systems and perfusion

System switches indicate whether a model is enabled. They do not mean the patient has TBI, shock or an arrhythmia. Perfusion rows combine current patient variables with diagnostic mirrors.

Display fieldUnit / formatMeaning and interpretation
SYSTEMS: TBIBooleanExtended TBI system enabled for the mission. In the screenshot it is true even though the selected patient has no TBI state.
SYSTEMS: CircBooleanExtended circulation model enabled.
SYSTEMS: RhythmBooleanExtended rhythm system enabled.
Shock0–1 severity mirrorDiagnostic shock-severity variable. It is intended to describe the Extended shock process, not the amount of blood lost. A zero/default mirror does not exclude hypovolemic shock.
MAP del / MAP deltammHg-equivalent estimateNet modeled support minus shock and calcium-related pressure penalties. The narrow renderer truncates the label to MAP del. This is a diagnostic estimate; the current model carries effects through heart rate and vascular resistance rather than adding this value to BP a second time.

05

Pressor response

Raw, Eff and Lost show the model’s estimated pressure-support response. Compare support with the actual BP trend. Acidosis and cold can reduce the response even while medication is reaching the patient.

Display fieldUnit / formatMeaning and interpretation
RawmmHg-equivalent supportCombined modeled infusion, bolus and surge support before the acidosis and cold reductions. Earlier delivery, volume and calcium gates may already have affected this number.
EffmmHg-equivalent supportSupport remaining after the acidosis and cold reductions. This is not a fluid volume.
LostmmHg-equivalent supportmax(Raw − Eff, 0). It measures modeled loss of response, not leaked drug mass.
Acid %% reductionAcidosis-related support reduction. It is not blood pH, the total acidosis score or the percentage of the dose lost.
Cold %% reductionHypothermia-related support reduction. Acid and Cold multiply their remaining fractions; simply adding their percentages overstates the combined loss.
InfDiagnostic support mirrorThe public infusion-support mirror read by this overlay. The current detailed support is stored in the circulation state; a default zero in this mirror is not proof that no infusion is active.
PDEDiagnostic support mirrorPush-dose epinephrine support mirror. PDE here means push-dose epinephrine, not a phosphodiesterase medication. The current bolus-support calculation is tracked separately in circulation state.
DistalLegacy surge mirrorFormer distal-site pressor-spike diagnostic. The current circulation calculation explicitly sets the location-only spike to zero. A distal IV does not itself turn norepinephrine into epinephrine; local leakage and vesicant injury remain separate concerns.
Reading the calculation

Effective support = Raw × (1 − Acid fraction) × (1 − Cold fraction). For example, Raw 20 with Acid 25% and Cold 20% gives 20 × 0.75 × 0.80 = 12, with 8 lost. These are diagnostic support units, not a dosing recommendation.

06

Acidosis and carbon dioxide

The acidosis scores are internal 0–1 burdens, not pH values. The rows explain whether poor perfusion, inadequate ventilation, saline exposure or low CPP is feeding that burden.

Display fieldUnit / formatMeaning and interpretation
Total0–1 scoreCombined metabolic and respiratory acidosis, capped at 1. Used by response and coagulation effects.
Metab0–1 scoreMetabolic acidosis burden, including the saline-related floor in the current model.
Resp0–1 scoreRespiratory acidosis burden responding to retained arterial CO₂.
Shock0–1 driverCurrent poor-perfusion fraction driving metabolic acidosis. This is different from the shock-severity mirror under Perfusion.
Vent0–1 deficitModeled ventilation deficit. A value of zero means no deficit in this calculation, not that the ventilator is off.
CPP0–1 driverContribution from inadequate cerebral perfusion to the acidosis model. This is not the mmHg CPP shown under TBI / ICP.
ShkMinMinutesAccumulated time in inadequate perfusion used by the onset model. It helps explain why a newly low pressure may precede a large metabolic burden.
HypMinMinutesHypoventilation dwell time used by CO₂ retention. It is not hypovolemia time or hypothermia duration.
AcidMAPmmHgThe pressure estimate fed into the acidosis driver, before the final support reductions. Arrest can force its base to zero. An absent circulation state also defaults this field to zero.
PaCO2mmHgModeled arterial CO₂. The usual initial value is 40.0, including the fallback used before a state is available.
EtCO2mmHg or n/aObserved end-tidal CO₂ from the common game getter when available. n/a means the overlay has no observed value; it does not mean a measured zero.
GapmmHgNonnegative arterial minus end-tidal CO₂ gap. If no observed EtCO₂ exists, the model can use its estimate for the gap. Read this with EtCO2 availability.
Exhaleyes / noWhether the model detects sufficient exhalation conditions. It is not the instantaneous inspiration/expiration phase of a waveform.
BVMyes / noWhether the acidosis model detects active manual ventilation. no is expected for an independently breathing patient; the red text alone does not mean treatment is required.
CO2 +mmHg/sCurrent modeled rate of PaCO₂ accumulation from inadequate ventilation.
CO2 −mmHg/sCurrent modeled PaCO₂-clearance rate. It is limited by the model’s normal PaCO₂ floor, so a positive rate does not guarantee the stored value is falling below normal.
hCl0–1 scoreHyperchloremic acidosis burden from the tracked normal-saline exposure. It is not hydrogen chloride concentration or a measured serum chloride.
NSmLTracked normal-saline exposure used by that model. This is not total bag volume or the amount currently in the crystalloid compartment.
ColdGMultiplierCold-related multiplier on metabolic acidosis accumulation. ×1 is baseline; higher values accelerate accumulation.
RecoverMultiplierCold-related multiplier on metabolic acidosis recovery. ×1 is baseline; lower values slow recovery.

Use the overlay to troubleshoot

Compare a short sequence of observations. A single screenshot may show defaults, a pending onset or values that update on different simulation ticks.

Display fieldUnit / formatMeaning and interpretation
Drug accepted, little immediate effectCheck onsetLook for accepted/pending, effective sedation or serum progression, bag flow and the patient’s actual response. Bag medication count does not include every injected drug.
Good SpO₂, unstable patientCheck delivery and perfusionCompare BP, Circ, Blood, breathing and player assessments. Debug oxygen saturation does not replace a reliable probe signal or prove adequate oxygen delivery.
Treatment visible only to one playerCheck ownershipCapture patient name, Owner, Local, Role, MP and net rev on both machines; note whether Pend remains nonzero.
Different HR and monitor behaviorCheck representationsCapture current HR, TgtHR, Native, Active and Visual together. Beat/QRS off are feature flags, not diagnoses.
Zero AcidMAP despite normal vitalsCheck active stateLook at the circulation tracker and whether the model has populated its state. Do not interpret an isolated default as a measured pressure.
Unexpected value or clipped labelKeep the full contextRecord the mod version, active settings, exact action, elapsed time and a screenshot of all columns. The debug-to-clipboard dump contains the fuller raw state for a technical report.
