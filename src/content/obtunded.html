Obtunded states

Recognize partial impairment, identify its cause and distinguish recovery from a brief response to stimulation.

What the player experiences

An obtunded player remains awake in the medical state system but has reduced awareness. Effects include fluctuating blur, dimming at the edge of vision, brief eyelid closures, muffled sound and altered speech when supported. Short lucid intervals can temporarily improve the effects.

The current state allows normal movement and weapon inputs. Starting a sprint while moving can cause a brief fall, with a default 35% chance per eligible sprint attempt. Simply entering the state does not force a particular posture. Actual medical unconsciousness takes over if the patient deteriorates.

Automatic entry and recovery

These checks apply to living players when physiological obtundation is enabled. The master option is off by default under Addon Options > ACM Extended: Trauma > 2. Obtundation. Either oxygenation or perfusion can trigger entry; recovery requires both to improve.

Assessment and treatment

  1. Check response, airway, breathing, pulse, blood pressure and SpO2. Look for hemorrhage and head injury.
  2. Correct the cause: control bleeding and restore perfusion, or correct airway and breathing problems.
  3. Reassess after treatment. A lucid interval, clearer speech or a brief wake-up response does not prove that oxygenation and circulation have recovered.
  4. Use the head injury reference for abnormal pupils or a worsening neurological examination.

Ammonia and fracture pressure awakening

A new ammonia inhalant use can rapidly end obtundation when vital signs are stable and no condition forces unconsciousness. It gives a default 20-second stimulus grace period. Persistent hypoxia or shock can still cause deterioration later.

Applying direct pressure over a fractured limb can immediately wake an eligible lightly unconscious or obtunded patient. The action adds severe pain, then checks stable vital signs and the same forced unconsciousness gate used by ACM wake-up actions. The probability scales with SpO2. It does not override deep sedation or another forced unconscious state.

The fracture stimulus has an 8-second per limb cooldown and an 8-second wake stimulus grace period by default. It adds pain without generating artificial fracture damage or bleeding. A woken patient remains lying until the appropriate get up action. Use pressure for bleeding control and account for this pain response when a fracture is present.
