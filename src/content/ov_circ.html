02

Medication response

Adenosine requires rapid systemic delivery for SVT conversion. Amiodarone, lidocaine and magnesium have different rhythm targets and timing. Read the matching card before judging whether the medication failed.

For delayed IV responses, check ACM Extended: Circulation > Medications > Require saline flush after push and examine the line. Increasing the amount drawn does not correct a closed or leaking line.

03

Scenario options

ACM: Circulation > Cardiac Arrest > Rhythm Deterioration Multiplier adjusts deterioration probability. ACM Extended: Systems > Hardcore > [HARDCORE] Cardiac Rhythms adds ROSC restrictions. The full cardiac rhythm guide lists default entry times, conversion responses and available tuning options.
