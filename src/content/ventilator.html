Ventilator Settings & Tips

Ventway Sparrow controls, delivered breath checks, oxygenation, pressure limits and power management in ACM Extended.

Start and reassess

  1. Assess the airway, chest, breathing and circulation. Connect the patient and confirm effective ventilation.
  2. Select the mode and patient weight. Check rate, volume or pressure target, FiO2, PEEP and the pressure limit.
  3. Confirm delivered VTe, minute ventilation, respiratory rate, airway pressure and SpO2 after breaths have begun.
  4. Reassess after every setting change, airway intervention, movement or alarm.

Ketamine sedation and nystagmus

Assess Pupils can show nystagmus when deep ketamine sedation is present. With plain descriptors, the report says the eyes are rapidly and involuntarily moving. With clinical descriptors, it adds “Nystagmus.”

The finding requires a living, unconscious patient who is not in cardiac arrest or under rocuronium paralysis. Current ketamine effect must reach the induction normalized threshold of 1 and exceed propofol plus midazolam effects combined. Analgesic ketamine alone does not meet this threshold.

This is a useful in game confirmation of ketamine dominant deep sedation when present. Its absence does not establish inadequate sedation: another sedative may dominate, or paralysis may suppress this observation. Continue assessing medication effect and ventilation. Existing injury related pupil findings remain in the same report.

Controls and measurements

VTi goes in; VTe comes back. Compare both when checking a breath. In the game, leak reduces expired volume relative to inspired volume, and delivered minute ventilation uses the expired volumes. Check the capnography patterns alongside these readings.

Oxygenation and shunt

Edema and blast lung create recruitable shunt. PEEP and inspiratory recruitment reduce that component. Pneumothorax and hemothorax create a fixed component that PEEP does not clear.

PEEP recruitment scales across 4–15 cmH2O. Above 16 cmH2O, an overdistension term grows toward its maximum at 24 cmH2O. FiO2 improves oxygenation toward the remaining shunt ceiling; it does not erase the shunt itself. At altitude, reduced ambient pressure also affects the calculation.

Recruitable component

Edema and blast lung: PEEP and inspiratory recruitment reduce the part of the shunt the game can reopen.

Fixed component

Pleural air and blood: assess decompression or drainage. Turning up PEEP does not remove these causes.

Residual oxygen ceiling

FiO2: raises oxygenation toward the remaining shunt ceiling. A saturation plateau can mean the cause still needs treatment.

Use oxygen delivery and flight physiology alongside the saturation reading.

High delivered volume can add stretch injury independently of the pressure dose branch. The calculation begins above 8 mL/kg when effective compliance is below 0.80 and scales with volume and compliance deficit. Reassess volume, pressure, obstruction and drainage together.

Gas trapping

The game compares expiratory time with an emptying time estimate of 1.8 seconds × effective compliance. Insufficient expiration accumulates auto-PEEP at up to 0.9 cmH2O per second. Adequate expiration clears it at 1.2 cmH2O per second.

The default auto-PEEP ceiling is 20 cmH2O and the GAS TRAPPING alarm threshold is 4 cmH2O. Auto-PEEP adds to pressure and can affect pressure limiting. Increase the expiratory share of I:E, lower rate when appropriate, and recheck delivered ventilation.

Battery and vehicle power

The turbine uses ambient air to ventilate; supplemental oxygen enriches the gas mixture. When battery drain is enabled, electrical charge limits operation.

LOW BATTERY appears at 25% and BATTERY CRITICAL at 10%. At zero, ventilation stops and manual support is required. Battery exchange takes about 6 seconds and is offered below 90% charge.
