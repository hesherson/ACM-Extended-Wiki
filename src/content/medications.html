Medication reference

Game-specific stock presentations, route classes, effect references, and timing for 33 medications and products.

What these values mean

An effect reference is a dose used to normalize the game’s medication model. An infusion registry target is a separate tracking value. Neither is a universal administration instruction. The graphs show one native record’s configured effect envelope; they do not predict every physiological effect or a whole running infusion.

Delivery rules

Route or actionRule
IV/IO native actionsRequire a configured native route. Cardiac epinephrine is restricted to IV/IO in the native path.
Prepared syringeUses a separate permissive gate for configured injectable sources. Being drawable does not establish an appropriate clinical use or a native action for that route.
Flush-required push, when enabledAdenosine, amiodarone, calcium chloride, calcium gluconate and epinephrine wait in the line until a saline flush.
Prepared infusionOnly the current allowed medications can be added to saline. Drug effects follow systemic delivered volume.
Local antidote infiltrationNative phentolamine and hyaluronidase infiltration exits through the local reversal path before systemic medication records are created.
Dead patientThe medication-effect callback exits without creating effects.
Basic ACE medication modeUses its separate basic-medication behavior. The detailed card values below describe the advanced path.

Interactions

Current exposureAdditional modeled effect
Opioid + propofol or midazolamRespiratory-rate and CO2-sensitivity reduction, with additional loss of peripheral resistance
Opioid + ketamineAdds a smaller respiratory interaction term than opioid plus propofol or midazolam
Propofol + midazolamAdds respiratory and vascular depression
Ketamine + midazolamAdds a smaller respiratory interaction term
Amiodarone + esmololAdds reduced heart rate and peripheral resistance
Reduced physiological reserveAmplifies the vascular interaction penalty
Systemic phentolamine from a prepared mixtureAdds bounded vasodilation and modest tachycardia

Interactions use current normalized medication exposure. They do not add raw milligram amounts together and do not directly assign a rhythm or force an arrest. Native rhythm and perfusion systems determine downstream deterioration.
