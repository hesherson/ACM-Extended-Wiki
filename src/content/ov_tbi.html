Head injury assessment

Follow neurological findings together with perfusion and breathing.

01

Recognize deterioration

Reassess consciousness, pupils, breathing and circulation after head injury. Rising intracranial pressure can produce a Cushing response, including a slower heart rate and altered blood pressure. Medication effects can also change consciousness or pupils, so review treatment history.

The clinical descriptor reference explains examination wording. See Traumatic brain injury for the broader system guide.
