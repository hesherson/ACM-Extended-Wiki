Ten modules for blood volume, oxygen, pain, rhythm, chest injury, wake-up, plot armor and full heal facilities.

Place, configure, reassess

Drop patient modules directly on the intended person. Place the fridge and training manikin at their intended world position. Confirm the resulting findings before trainees begin.

One change at a time

Most injury modules act immediately and remove their placement marker. TBI, IV and fridge modules open a setup dialog. A clear or wake-up action only changes its named state; use the reset reference before repeating a scenario.

Place IV: build access before the exercise

  1. Drop Place IV on the casualty.
  2. Choose Left arm, Right arm, Left leg, Right leg or Neck (EJ).
  3. Choose Upper, Middle or Lower for a limb; Left (EJ) or Right (EJ) for the neck.
  4. Select 14G, 16G, 18G or 20G and confirm. The defaults are Left arm, Middle, 16G.
  5. Open the access screen to verify the catheter hub and select that site when attaching a bag or administering medication.

Rows that do not apply to the chosen limb are greyed and cannot remain selected. Switching to the neck selects the left EJ if the prior selection was a limb height. This module places IV or EJ access; its dialog does not place an IO. See the IV/IO decision guide when planning separate medication and fluid routes.

Blood fridge: placement and resupply

Choose ACM Extended > Place Blood Fridge in Zeus, place it at the intended supply point, then configure the popup and select Place. Cancel closes the setup without spawning a fridge.

Finite exercise supply

Set the required stock and choose Regenerates: No. Every withdrawal reduces the remaining stock.

Recurring supply point

Choose Yes and a positive interval. Each fridge refills to its own configured stock when its interval expires.

Time acceleration matters

The interval follows mission date/time progression. Twenty four in game hours can pass faster than 24 real hours.

Restocking restores the configured load; it does not add another full load to whatever remains. The timer starts after placement and follows the individual fridge's elapsed interval. It is not a shared midnight refill.

Taking blood and reading the contents screen

  1. Approach within the fridge's 4 m ACE interaction distance.
  2. Open the world interaction menu on the fridge. Its door opens while players interact and closes after the last user stops.
  3. Select Blood Fridge > Check Contents to inspect the stocked types, unit counts, total milliliters and restock setting.
  4. Select Blood Fridge > Take Blood Unit, then an available type. Each action takes one 500 mL unit.
  5. Confirm that the unit reached your inventory. If there is no room, the inventory helper can place it on the ground. Store blood inside a working cooler when continued cold storage is needed.

Zero stock types remain visible in grey on the contents screen. The Take Blood Unit submenu lists only types with stock remaining. Check Contents reads the stock when opened; reopen it after withdrawals to check the latest counts.

Blood remains stocked in the fridge without a loose bag aging timer. Once withdrawn, the blood cold chain rules apply. Carrying a cooler alone does not guarantee a loose bag is protected: confirm the blood is stored inside it.

Placement module versus fridge models: Place Blood Fridge is the selectable placement tool. Blood Fridge (Stocked) and Blood Fridge (Open) are the internal closed/open object models used by that tool, not separate exposed Zeus placement variants.

Eden attributes and regeneration details

The module's Eden/scripted path supports individual counts for all eight blood groups. Those attributes are O− 2, O+ 6 and zero for A−, A+, B−, B+, AB− and AB+ by default.

Restock periodically defaults to enabled. Regen interval (in game minutes) defaults to 1,440. A value of 0 disables regeneration; −1 uses the addon option interval. This is separate from the live Zeus popup, whose count and hour/minute sliders open at the displayed defaults.

A per fridge interval overrides the addon interval. The blood cold chain system must be enabled for the fridge's automatic door/restock tick to run. A zero interval stops actual regeneration even if a contents footer describes an interval of 0 h 0 min.

Instructor driven simulation: the panel sets training values and selected live medical states. It is not a free running ordinary casualty. The scenario labels describe the exercise; they do not establish that every named disease process or ECG feature has a separate simulation behind it.

Timed scenarios

Times below use the default pace of 1. Each stage is checked about once per second. A pace multiplier of 2 doubles the stage times; 0.5 halves them.

ScenarioScheduled progressionInstructor focus
ACS > VF arrestStart with tachycardia; hypotensive stage at 45 s; VT at 90 s; VF at 120 s.Rhythm recognition and the transition from a perfusing rhythm to arrest.
Tension pneumo > PEAPneumothorax at the start; tension at 40 s; PEA at 85 s.Chest assessment, decompression and perfusion reassessment.
Hemorrhage > PEABilateral leg junctional bleeds at the start; worsening shock at 40 s; PEA at 90 s.Hemorrhage control, access and resuscitation planning.
Hyperkalemia > VFSinus at the start; bradycardia at 40 s; VF at 80 s.Rhythm progression. The timed script does not itself set a potassium concentration.
Rising ICP > herniationRaised training ICP at the start; Cushing stage at 45 s; coma/herniation at 95 s; asystole at 135 s.Neurological trend and deterioration. This sequence is separate from the ordinary casualty TBI progression model.
Hypoxia > bradyasystoleAirway collapse and hypoxia at the start; bradycardia at 40 s; asystole at 80 s.Airway treatment, ventilation and reassessment.
Septic shock > PEAFever and shock pattern targets at the start; lower pressure at 50 s; PEA at 100 s.Recognition of deterioration and preparation for arrest.

STOP SCENARIO stops future scripted stages. It does not undo injuries or values already applied. The sequence advances by elapsed time, so treatment does not automatically cancel its next scheduled deterioration. Stop or reset it when the exercise calls for a different course.

The manikin has training specific death protection. By default, 240 accumulated pulseless seconds without CPR trigger the simulated death/reset sequence; active CPR pauses that clock. The configured death display lasts 3 seconds before reset. A genuinely dead manikin is replaced through the respawn path.

05

Resetting a scenario

Clear the named condition

Clear TBI removes the TBI model and its offsets. Clear Pulmonary Edema removes fluid overload. Place Induce / Clear Obtundation again to clear a manual obtunded episode.

Stop scheduled deterioration

For Megacode Kelly, use STOP SCENARIO to halt future stages or RESET to restore the training baseline. DONE only closes the panel.

Verify the next starting state

Check blood compartments, bleeding, rhythm, oxygenation, TBI, blast lung, medication effects and attached devices. Record the starting state before the next attempt.

A module can report that its action was applied while another condition still drives abnormal findings. Match the reset to the finding you are trying to remove, then reassess the whole casualty.
