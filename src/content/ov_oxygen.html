Oxygen readings

Saturation, delivered breaths and circulation answer different questions.

01

Read the measurements together

MeasurementWhat to assess
SpO2Oxygen saturation. Interpret it alongside airway patency, chest injury and delivered oxygen.
Respiratory rate and delivered volumeWhether breaths are occurring and moving enough gas. A set ventilator rate alone does not establish effective ventilation.
EtCO2 and its waveformExhaled CO2 and breath detection. Check the airway, ventilation and circulation when the waveform or value changes.
Pulse and blood pressureWhether blood is circulating. Saturation does not replace a perfusion assessment.
02

Choose the next assessment

Use the airway and chest guide for obstructed or ineffective breathing, ventilator guide for respiratory support, and rhythm guide when a pulse is absent or unreliable. The examination reference explains the displayed findings.
